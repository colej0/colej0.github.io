FAQ

Is it live today?

Signups and trials are open now. We're onboarding businesses in small batches while our Google Business Profile integration completes approval. Until your account is connected, nothing is posted and your trial doesn't run down.

Will it post something embarrassing?

Auto-posting is limited to positive reviews. On Pro, every 1–3★ reply waits for your approval. You can pause auto-posting anytime.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You connect through Google's own sign-in screen and can revoke access from your Google account at any time.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. If you don't add a payment method before the trial ends, the subscription simply won't continue.

Can my agency resell it?

Yes — white-label per-location pricing is available. Join the list above and mention you're an agency.
